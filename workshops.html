How to book

For a group,
a business, or just yourself.

Every workshop can run in three ways. Say which one you want when you get in touch.

🏠
At your place

Raffaella brings the ingredients and equipment to your kitchen — home, office, wherever there's a worktop.

🎉
At your event

A workshop as entertainment: a birthday, a team day, a hen party, a client evening. Adapted to the room you've booked.

🍝
In our place

A partner kitchen in Schiedam or Rotterdam, set up and ready — the simplest option if you don't need your own venue.
