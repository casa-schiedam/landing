01

The menu is yours

Written after Raffaella knows the occasion and the guests. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and allergies are designed in, not apologised for.

02

Everything is included

Shopping, cooking, serving and the clean-up. Wine isn't included — Raffaella isn't licensed to sell it — but she's happy to suggest a pairing for the menu, and extra service staff can be added on request.

03

One clear price

Quoted before anything is booked, invoiced properly if you need that. No surprises at the end of the evening.
