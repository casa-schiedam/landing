01 · At your home, for small groups

Metti una sera a cena.
A meal that tells a story.

Book Raffa & Vale for an aperitivo, a lunch or a dinner at your place. Every meal has a theme and a narrative — a region, a memory, a season, a family recipe — told course by course while you eat. Guests can ask, taste, lend a hand: it's an evening you take part in, not one that just gets served to you.

Aperitivo

Small plates and drinks, standing or sitting, built around one theme — the street food of Naples, say, or a summer on the coast. Short stories with every bite, and the fried things straight from the pan. Raffa — confirm minimum

03 · Made to measure

Sartoria del Gusto.
Tailored to you.

Some ideas don't fit a format — and they shouldn't have to. Sartoria del Gusto is a custom-made experience, cut and sewn around your wishes like a tailored suit: the place, the people, the story, the food. Tell Raffa what you have in mind and she'll design it with you, from the first fitting to the finished evening.

Shopping, cooking, serving and the clean-up. Wine isn't included — we aren't licensed to sell it — but we're happy to suggest a pairing for the menu, and extra service staff can be added on request.
